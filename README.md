# Panchayt_app
Panchayat – Your Community, Connected 🏡

Panchayat is a modern, smart, and responsive community management platform designed to connect residents and make neighbourhood management easier.

### ✨ Key Features

* 🏠 Dashboard: View community updates and important statistics.

* 📢 Complaints: Submit, search, filter, and track community complaints.

* 🚨 Emergency Contacts: Access important emergency contact numbers.

* 🛠️ Local Services: Explore plumbers, electricians, cleaning, laundry, tutors, and more.

* 📖 Society Rules: View community guidelines and regulations.

* 🤖 Panchayat AI: Get quick answers to common community questions.

* 📅 Notices & Events: Stay updated with community announcements.

* 🏢 Facilities: Explore shared facilities and their information.

* 🌙 Dark & Light Mode: Switch between beautiful themes.

* 📱 Responsive Design: Smooth experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

### 🛠️ Technologies Used

* HTML5

* CSS3

* JavaScript

* Font Awesome

* Google Fonts

### 🎯 Project Goal

To make community management smarter, easier, faster, and more accessible by bringing essential neighbourhood services together in one platform.
